# About

I work on post-training and agentic systems, with a particular interest in what happens when language models leave the clean abstraction of a single turn.

My current questions sit near the intersection of **LLM agents**, **temporal reasoning**, and **embodied intelligence**: How should a model represent elapsed time? How can it remain responsive while reasoning? What belongs in the model, and what belongs in the surrounding harness?

This site is a public research notebook. I use it to develop technical arguments, connect papers across fields, and record ideas that are useful before they are polished enough to become a formal paper.

## What I write about

- model-side temporal representations and time-aware post-training;
- event-driven alternatives to strictly turn-based agents;
- belief state, asynchronous observations, and embodied System 2;
- paper readings where the implementation details matter as much as the headline result.

## A note on scope

The views here are personal. Posts focus on general research ideas and public information; they do not describe confidential systems or unpublished organizational results.
